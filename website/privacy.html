Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Ram Murti Jewellers (“we”, “us”) runs the website rmj.co.in, our shop’s WhatsApp numbers and our in-shop software. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Website. You can use rmj.co.in without an account. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.
  • WhatsApp messages. When you message us on WhatsApp, or ask for rate updates, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, the name on your WhatsApp profile if shared, and the messages you send us.
  • Rate update list. If you subscribe (by sending START, DAILY or WEEKLY), or you are a customer of our shop, we keep your name and mobile number to send you gold and silver rate updates and occasional offers, together with whether each message was delivered.
  • Shop records. When you buy, sell, repair or pledge jewellery with us, we keep the details needed for billing, repairs, legal and tax purposes.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and send the rates, photos or quotes you ask for.
  • To send rate updates and offers on WhatsApp if you subscribed or are our customer — you can stop them at any time.
  • To run our shop: billing, repairs, customer accounts, and meeting legal and tax requirements.

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that make these services work — for example Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) to deliver WhatsApp messages, and our hosting providers — and when the law requires it.

WhatsApp

Messages to and from our official WhatsApp number are delivered through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform and are also subject to WhatsApp’s privacy policy. Reply STOP (or tap “Stop updates”) to any rate message and you will not receive them again. We keep a note that you opted out so you are never added back by mistake.

How long we keep it

Rate-update subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. Shop and billing records are kept for as long as Indian law requires. Website logs are kept only for a short period by our hosting provider.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you at any time:

  • Send DELETE MY DATA on WhatsApp to +91 97818 00888, or
  • email rammurtijewellers@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and the mobile number concerned.

We will confirm and delete your details from our rate-update list and messaging records within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (such as invoices) are kept only for the period the law requires and are not used for anything else. If you only want to stop rate updates, reply STOP — that works instantly.

Security

Access to our records is limited to our staff through password-protected accounts, and data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
