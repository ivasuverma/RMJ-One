RMJ One — Privacy Policy

RMJ One is the private business management app of Ram Murti Jewellers, used by the shop's owner and staff at app.rmj.co.in. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

Business records entered by the shop's own staff: employee attendance and payroll, repair and stock records, cash book and ledger entries, and photos and scanned documents (such as customer KYC, bills and statements) that staff upload.

Google account access

The app asks for one Google permission, drive.file. It lets the app create and manage only the files it created itself in the shop's Google Drive, in a folder named "RMJ One Documents", as an off-site backup of uploaded documents and photos and of the database. The app cannot see or open any other file in that Google account.

How the data is used

Only to run the shop's operations and keep backups. Data is stored on the shop's own server and in the shop's own Google Drive. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or given to any third party.

Who can see it

Only the shop's owner and the staff members the owner has given access to, each limited to the sections the owner enables for them.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept for as long as the shop needs them. The owner can delete documents and photos in the app, which also removes their copy from Google Drive. The owner can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings, and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ask the shop owner, Ram Murti Jewellers.
