RMJ One

The business management app of Ram Murti Jewellers

RMJ One is the private internal application that Ram Murti Jewellers uses to run its day-to-day operations. It is used by the shop's owner and staff on their phones and computers, and is not offered to the public.

What the app does

  • Staff: attendance, leave, payroll and tasks.
  • Jewellery work: repair tracking, stock in/out, karigar (artisan) accounts, gold loans.
  • Accounts: cash book and ledgers.
  • Documents and photos: staff scan or photograph documents (customer KYC, bills, statements) and repair photos, which are stored in the shop's own Google Drive.

How the app uses Google

RMJ One asks the shop owner to connect a Google account with a single permission, drive.file. This lets the app create and manage only the files it creates itself, in a folder named "RMJ One Documents" (and "RMJ One Backups" for system backups), in the shop's own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in that Google account. Google data is used only to store and retrieve those documents and backups for the shop, and is never shared with anyone else.

Signing in

Access is by invitation only: the owner creates accounts for staff and decides which sections each person can use.
